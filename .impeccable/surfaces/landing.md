# Public landing context

The white landing uses the accepted Gabay artwork, oversized AGAP wordmark, four-chapter scroll story, and scroll-linked fictional screenshot gallery. Mobile, short-viewport, and reduced-motion alternatives remain static. Installation is browser-controlled and online-only; workspace authentication remains required.
