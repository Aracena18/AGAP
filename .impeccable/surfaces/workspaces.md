# Member and staff workspace context

Workspaces use the accepted green/white/yellow Gabay identity, shared Google Sans/Material Symbols, compact staff navigation, and member task navigation. Keep financial evidence, currentness, unavailable states, and the next action readable. Phone controls and forms must preserve keyboard/focus, 320px reflow, and unsaved/uncertain command guards.

[design.md](../../design.md) is the sole visual/interaction authority, including the workspace direction in section 16 and mobile member behavior in section 20. [Product requirements](../../docs/AGAP_Product_Solution.md), [team status](../../Assignment.md), and [verification](../../docs/AGAP_Verification.md) define behavior and evidence. This file introduces no parallel palette, components, or design rules.
