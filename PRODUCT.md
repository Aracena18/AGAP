# AGAP product context

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Cooperative staff prepare and review loans; members use their own account and assisted paths. The public landing page primarily introduces the fictional product demonstration to hackathon visitors, as confirmed by the user.

## Product Purpose

AGAP connects relevant evidence, household needs, repayment scenarios and accountable human decisions. [Product solution](docs/AGAP_Product_Solution.md) is the authority for target capabilities; [Assignment.md](Assignment.md) records actual progress.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use the existing React/TypeScript/Vite application, shared controls and deterministic calculations. Missing information is unavailable, never zero. AI drafts are reviewable and have no financial or decision authority. The browser demonstration is fictional; installed access remains online-only. Existing staff/member authentication remains required.

## Brand Commitments

The user approved the existing Gabay firefly artwork and green/white/yellow identity for the landing page. The follow-up browser comments explicitly request a white landing and an oversized AGAP wordmark. Existing workspaces retain their current identity. [design.md](design.md) is the sole visual/interaction authority; this file introduces no parallel design rules.

## Evidence on Hand

Canonical fictional fixtures are in `src/demo/cases.ts`; historical independent oracles are in `docs/AGAP_Product_Freeze.md`. The selected accepted artwork and its provenance are recorded in [UI-07 verification](docs/AGAP_Landing_Verification.md). No customer endorsements or measured impact claims are supplied.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow design.md's WCAG 2.2 AA target, 44px standalone controls, keyboard/focus behavior, 320px reflow and reduced-motion support.
