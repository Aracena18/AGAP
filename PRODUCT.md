# AGAP product context

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Cooperative staff prepare, investigate, and review loans. Members use their own account or an assisted application path. The public landing page introduces the fictional prototype to hackathon visitors.

## Product Purpose

AGAP connects source-linked evidence, household needs, repayment scenarios, accountable human decisions, and ongoing support. The [product solution](docs/AGAP_Product_Solution.md) defines target capabilities; [team status](Assignment.md) and [verification](docs/AGAP_Verification.md) distinguish supplied behavior from pending work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use the existing React/TypeScript/Vite application, shared controls, and deterministic calculations. Missing information is unavailable, never zero. AI drafts are reviewable and have no financial or decision authority. Demonstration records and money movements are fictional. Server member/staff access is distinct from browser demo roles; installation does not provide offline access.

## Brand Commitments

Gabay, the firefly character, and the green/white/yellow identity appear on the white landing page and member/staff workspaces. The landing uses an oversized AGAP wordmark. [design.md](design.md) is the sole visual/interaction authority; this file defines no parallel design rules.

## Evidence on Hand

Canonical fictional cases are in `src/demo/cases.ts`; independent historical arithmetic is in [the calculation reference](docs/AGAP_Product_Freeze.md). Artwork inventory and provenance are in the [design library](design-library/README.md). No customer endorsement or measured borrower-outcome improvement is established.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the design system's WCAG 2.2 AA target, 44px standalone controls, keyboard/focus behavior, 320px reflow, and reduced-motion support. Automated checks cover tested states; representative-user evaluation remains required.
