Interaction rules

A little motion goes a long way.

  • Use purposeful moments, not endless loops.
  • Keep financial figures steady, readable and selectable.
  • Never make debt, rejection or warnings feel celebratory.
  • Respect reduced motion and touch accessibility.

Gabay is a supportive guide—not an AI approver. Every critical message must remain understandable without animation or sound.
