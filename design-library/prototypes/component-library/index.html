01 / FOUNDATIONS

A quieter, more confident identity

The palette is deliberately restrained. Dark green carries actions and headings; yellow is an accent, not a warning substitute or an approval signal.

Trust before decoration

White space and readable financial details are the priority. Avoid unnecessary gradients, glass effects, and stacked shadows.

05 / GABAY INTEGRATION

Personality with a purpose

Gabay should introduce, explain, and support—while staying out of the way during review, money entry, and high-consequence decisions.

Display rules

  • Lead with the user's task and next action, not the character.
  • Provide plain-language guidance independent of animation.
  • Keep animations low intensity and respect reduced motion.

06 / IMPLEMENTATION RULES

One system, two workspaces

Member and staff interfaces share the design foundations, but the staff experience uses less illustration and higher information density.

Member portal

Warm and guided

Short explanations, guided steps, approachable empty states, larger touch targets, and occasional Gabay moments.

Careful language

“Application under review” is accurate. “You're approved!” is not, until a reviewer records it.

Staff workspace

Focused and precise

Compact tables, explicit evidence status, review history, accessible source links, and restrained feedback—usually no mascot illustration.

Design contract

Keep existing authority

Production UI rules live in AGAP's design.md, with real values in existing styles. Approve changes before migration.

Use existing icon set

Adopt the repository's Material Symbols Rounded icon exports, instead of adding another icon library.

Test real states

320px reflow, 200% zoom, keyboard/focus, saving uncertainty, offline error recovery, and reduced motion.
