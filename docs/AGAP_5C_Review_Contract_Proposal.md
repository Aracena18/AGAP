Character concerns are restricted to documented repayment-related evidence under the approved rubric. No credit file means uncertainty or approved alternative evidence, not dishonesty. Collateral not required by a product has a policy-based not-applicable state; missing required ownership/valuation evidence is not that state. Capacity keeps actual dated shortages and essential needs visible even when other category scores are high.

## Proposed score units and unavailable states

Money continues to use canonical integer centavos. A rubric score needs a **separate non-money value**: propose recorded integer `scoreBasisPoints` in 0–10000, tied to a trace; 10000 represents 100 percent of the defined rubric range. This is proposed serialization precision, not a numerical lending policy, model confidence, probability or claim of measurement precision. Aaron/institutional reviewers must agree a compatible scale before canonical encoding if their actual rubric requires different precision. Compute weighted intermediate results exactly with approved rounding; do not use LLM arithmetic or floating-point money.

An unavailable score carries an explicit reason such as rubric-unconfigured, missing-required-evidence, unresolved-discretionary-rating, stale-basis or rule-based-not-applicable; it has **no numeric score field**. A supported zero remains a recorded score of zero. Every category remains present, including unavailable categories. Required missing criteria cannot be assigned zero, silently removed from the denominator, or converted to not-applicable. Evidence coverage uses a separately defined value/denominator and is never copied into the rubric score.

Rubric applicability, evidence/source verification, candidate review, calculation availability and final lending decision remain independent dimensions. No configured aggregation means no overall score field/value. A configured aggregate must retain its rule, included/excluded criteria and limitations, and cannot override a critical gap, cash shortage, approval restriction or required exception. No repayment/default probability or `percentHonest`/AI-confidence field is proposed. Existing emergency-fund EBM/logistic research has no path into these records.

## Proposed operations and saved-version rules

| Operation | Required input/result | Ownership and guards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Load applicable rubric | Authorized application/product/as-of scope → current approved rubric, known not-applicable rule or unconfigured/unavailable result | Aaron; public five-Cs educational descriptions do not supply operative weights. | Prepare candidate findings | Minimum authorized immutable basis/rubric and source context → draft per-C mappings/questions with provenance | Aaron credit/evidence owns prompts/validation; Benedict's QWN-03 artifact uses the one adapter. Capture basis in a short authorized transaction, run AI outside DB/financial locks, then recheck before saving; changed inputs produce stale/renewed-review state |
| Review criterion finding | Authorized staff, expected finding/basis versions, criterion decision and reason, stable command key → preserved review record/receipt | Aaron; verify the actual rule/evidence and discretionary reviewer scope. Client/model-supplied reviewer IDs, verified flags or point totals are not authoritative |
| Calculate rubric run | Current fixed basis, approved rubric and accepted/confirmed criteria → checked per-C results, issues and complete trace | Aaron's deterministic service; unaccepted discretionary criteria block the affected reviewed score. Validate all source/offer/rubric versions before committing. Do not invent a new household calculator |
| Explain/prepare handoff | Current checked run/trace and authorized original sources, reviewed finding state, officer recommendation and stable key → saved source-linked explanation/handoff | Benedict; validate supplied references and figures, retain pending matters, use template/manual fallback if AI is absent or unsupported. Preparation does not call the final decision command |
| Inspect/review final case | Authorized officer/reviewer reads the same immutable handoff/current-basis checks → existing human recommendation/decision path with actual reasons and permitted exceptions | Benedict with Robert's shared wiring; preserve preparer/reviewer separation and conflict guards. High rubric score never grants authority or bypasses missing required evidence |

Each module writes its own records through the existing staff transaction boundary; source/member access is rechecked. Same-key/same-body retries preserve one immutable run/review/handoff; conflicting key reuse and stale expected versions use the existing command/precondition failures. No service-unavailable path produces a fake score, empty successful review or approval. Original documents, member statements, independent verification and actual decisions remain distinguishable.

Source corrections, newly adopted household facts, selected terms, rubric/criterion/approval changes or expired validity invalidate affected pending findings/runs/handoffs. Compare exact bindings on read, review and final authorization as well as processing invalidation events; an event delay cannot make an old score current. Renew review from a new basis while preserving historical score/decision snapshots. Reminder preferences or an unrelated voluntary goal edit do not change a loan basis by themselves.

The first panel is staff-scoped. A member's existing plain-language explanation/correction route remains; no public ranking or unrestricted access to CI/staff notes is added. Personal data needed for the declared review is proportionate, source-bound and access-controlled. Do not add gender/appearance/language/personality proxies or let irrelevant counterfactual changes affect ratings.
