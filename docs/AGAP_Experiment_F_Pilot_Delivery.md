## Connected behavior

The 0.4.0 contract adds generated qualitative review, priority/escalation/notices
and fictional lifecycle gateways. Ordinary 0.2/0.3 record meanings and legacy
snapshot readers remain intact. V4 decisions retain complete dated assessments
and multiple cash dates, with horizon net distinct from monthly segment values.

The staff queue and summary use the same readiness rules as consequential
decision/release checks. Source review resolves exact cleared source coordinates
server-side; untrusted drafts require human checking. Material concern priority
requires human substantiation. Ranking precedes snapshot-bound paging. In-app
acknowledgment does not clear a concern. Review generations have persisted leases
and completion receipts; interrupted work can be reconciled into a manual check.

The existing decision, member acceptance, principal-only servicing, actual GL,
loan reads and support services are registered behind `FICTIONAL_PILOT_ENABLED`.
The explicit fictional product has zero interest/fees/grace and exact scheduled
principal repayments. It does not replace Ana's interest-bearing example or
support partial/general servicing by assumption. Normal OIDC/MFA and separate
people remain required. Settlement evidence is server-generated simulation only.

The small pilot serializes organization material transactions after identity
guards and before financial/application locks. Evidence workers and policy/
retirement operators take the same guard. This is a conservative fictional-pilot
throughput choice, not a scalable production concurrency claim. Financial
posting, allocations, receipts and history remain one atomic transaction.
