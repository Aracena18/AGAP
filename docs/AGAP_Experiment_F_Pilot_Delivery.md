and completion receipts; interrupted work can be reconciled into a manual check.

loan reads and support services are registered behind `FICTIONAL_PILOT_ENABLED`.

people remain required. Settlement evidence is server-generated simulation only.

The small pilot serializes organization material transactions after identity
guards and before financial/application locks. Evidence workers and policy/
retirement operators take the same guard. This is a conservative fictional-pilot
throughput choice, not a scalable production concurrency claim. Financial
posting, allocations, receipts and history remain one atomic transaction.
