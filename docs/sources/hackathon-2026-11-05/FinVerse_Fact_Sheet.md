# Fact Sheet: FinVerse **Partner: Davao Coca-Cola Plant Employees Cooperative Multipurpose Cooperative**

### **1\. Key Facts**

| Item | Answer |
| ----- | ----- |
| *Team* | FinVerse, University of Mindanao. Robert Jhon D. Aracena (Software Developer), Aaron Jalapon Arquillano (Product Manager), John Benedict Bongcac (Machine Learning Developer), and Earl Jonas Tigno (Software Developer). |
| *Partner FSP info* | Primary multipurpose cooperative in Davao City, Philippines [\[1\]](https://cda.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/For-posting_2024-data.pdf).  |
| *Problem* | Loan repayments can leave cooperative members short on essentials even when they pay on time. Staff need to see where cash runs short before recommending a loan. |
| *Partner interest beyond the letter* | DCCPEC staff discussed their loan-review process with FinVerse and shared forms for the prototype. A written pilot-readiness of the prototype is the next milestone. |
| *Constraint (Part B)* | DCCPEC staff have limited time to reconcile scattered evidence and decide which applications need attention first if there’s a lot of applicants for review.  |
| *Impact Chain* | AGAP surfaces evidence and cash-flow gaps in a priority queue → staff clarify records and compare permitted repayment options before separate human approval → fewer members are expected to skip essentials to repay. |
| *Who benefits* | DCCPEC loan applicants whose proposed repayments could leave them short on essentials, including members who pay on time. |
| *Most likely harm \+ Safeguard* | **Harm**: Incomplete records or an unsuitable buffer could delay or exclude members. **Safeguard:** staff help members correct or clarify records and request another review. Explain total costs and trade-offs. An agreed buffer under cooperative rules is planned**.** |
| *Group that could be left out* | Applicants with irregular earnings or limited documentation. |
| *Who pays, and the price model* | Proposed subscription paid by the cooperative after the pilot. Price and willingness to pay are untested. |
| *Other FSPs with the same problem* | Historical cases at Ma-a Parish Multipurpose Cooperative (2017) and BATELEC II Employees Multi-Purpose Cooperative (2012) document credit-review and repayment problems. [\[2\]](https://www.ijbea.com/papers/journals/1/articles/83/submission/83-1-148-1-2-20200116.pdf), [\[3\]](https://library.cvsu.edu.ph/cgi-bin/koha/opac-retrieve-file.pl?id=6a53a3700d53517123493b3d05ba209f) |
| *Reach in 3 years: Low-bound, expected impact and High-bound*  | Team scenarios for potential beneficiaries. Applicants are cumulative unique people per co-op, assuming two active years at pilot volumes. Low: 1 × 200 × 40% × 0% \= 0 Expected: 2 × 400 × 60% × 10% \= 48 High: 3 × 600 × 80% × 20% \= 288\. Factors: co-ops × applicants × review share × benefit share. |
| *Who continues, and the 90-day milestone* | Robert (coordination) and Earl (measurement) will seek a written DCCPEC pilot-readiness decision by 4 February 2027 (90 days after 6 November 2026), covering scope, KPI protocol, data handling and a named staff owner. |
| *AI disclosure* | ChatGPT for drafting and editing, Codex for development, and Google Gemini for assistance with model development. |

### **3\. Impact estimate, 12-month pilot (Financial health impact)**

| Input | Low | Expected | High | Source or Rationale |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Planned AGAP-assisted applicants | 100 | 200 | 300 | Proposed pilot capacity: 2, 4, or 6 reviews/week × 50 weeks. |
| National benchmark: borrowers reporting repayment difficulty | 34% | 30% | 20% | 34% of Filipino borrowers reported difficulty repaying their loans \[4\]. |
| Indicative applicants potentially experiencing repayment difficulty | 34 |  68 | 102 | Planned reviews × 34% national proxy; not DCCPEC-specific. |
| Annual change per beneficiary | 1 | 1 | 1 | Each beneficiary is counted once if they avoid at least one repayment-related shortfall during the 12-month pilot. |

**Expected planning case:** 200 × 34% \= **68 potential repayment-difficulty cases reached** under the national proxy.

**Biggest uncertainty:** National borrower statistics may not represent DCCPEC applicants. Whether AGAP reduces essential-needs shortfalls requires follow-up measurement

**Fictional demo**  Ana has PHP 2,000 left for the month but reaches −PHP 2,000 before payday. A later due date allowed in the scenario raises the minimum to PHP 1,000 at equal loan cost, still PHP 500 below an illustrative PHP 1,500 buffer. Cash flow covers November 2026\. Loan cost covers 12 months.

**Demo status**  Queue, review summary and escalation verified on three fictional cases with simulated AI concerns. Buffer and preference capture, interactive expense shocks and member outcome tracking are planned.
