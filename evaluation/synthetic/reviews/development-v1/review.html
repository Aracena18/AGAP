How to review

Read the complete source, then compare the candidate answer and rationale. Check the amount or missing state, cash date, recurrence, relevance, next action and citation. Copy review-worksheet.json separately to record disagreements and corrections. The worksheet is not release approval.

Training is practice; validation guides development; holdout is a protected final exam. This pack supplies public development material only.

  • Coverage tags are author annotations, not proof of sufficient coverage.
  • Unique household/scenario/template counts are not independent statistical sample sizes. Connected groups must remain together under the existing splitter.
  • Expected answers are authored development candidates awaiting independent review; supported does not mean independently verified.
  • No model has been executed or evaluated by this pack.
